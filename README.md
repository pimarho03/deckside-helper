# Deckside Helper

The free program that connects your Mac or Windows PC to **Deckside**, the control deck for iPad.

Deckside turns your iPad into a touch control deck: tap a button and your computer opens an app, presses a shortcut or changes the volume. The helper is the small part that runs on the computer and does what the iPad asks.

Website: https://deckside-d117f.web.app

## Install

Your iPad and your computer must be on the same Wi-Fi.

**Mac**

1. Open the zip. You get "Deckside Helper".
2. Drag it into your Applications folder and open it.
3. A small grid icon appears at the top right of the screen. Click it, then "Connect my iPad".
4. The Mac asks for Accessibility permission so the helper can press shortcuts. Turn it on when asked.

**Windows**

1. Unzip the file somewhere that stays, for example `C:\Deckside Helper`.
2. Double-click `DecksideAgent.exe`.
3. Windows says "unknown publisher": click More info, then Run anyway.
4. A Deckside Helper icon appears near the clock. Double-click it, then "Connect my iPad".

**iPad**

Open Deckside and scan the code your computer shows.

## Private by design

The helper talks to your iPad directly over your own Wi-Fi. No account, no ads, no tracking.
